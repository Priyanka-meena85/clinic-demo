Privacy Policy (DPDP Act Compliant)

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the patient name, 10-digit mobile number, age, and consultation notes required to book OPD slots, issue queue tokens, and deliver prescriptions/lab reports.

2. How We Use Your Number

Your mobile number is strictly used for appointment confirmations, 15-minute pre-turn WhatsApp alerts, and digital prescription/report access. We never sell or share patient data with third-party marketers.

3. Role-Based Access & BOLA Protection

Medical records are isolated per Patient ID. Patients can access only their own and linked family profiles; receptionists cannot view clinic financials, and doctors view only their assigned consultations.
