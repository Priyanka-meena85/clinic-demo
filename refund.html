Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Advance Token Cancellation Rules

• 2+ hours before slot: 100% full refund of ₹100 UPI Advance.
• 30 minutes to 2 hours before slot: 50% refund (₹50).
• No-Show / less than 30 minutes: Non-refundable.

2. Doctor Leave / Failed Payment Auto-Refund

If a doctor cancels OPD or a payment is debited without token generation, 100% auto-refund is initiated to the original UPI/source within 3–5 business days, with an instant WhatsApp alert.
